Flush secret
You have a suited pair hand (5♦ Q♦) [1/16]:
 - 2/17 (~1/9) to get flush draw on the flop.
You have flush draw on the flop, turn:
 - 1/3 -> 1/5 to get flush on the river.
 - 2/7 to get flush on the river (your oppenent has same suits).
Recomendation: Don't pay a lot for the flop


Straight Secret
You have connectors hand (7♦ 8♥) [1/17]:
 - 3/14 (~2/9) to get straight draw on the flop.
 - 1/13 to get double straight draw on the flop.
You have double straight draw on the flop, turn:
 - 1/4 -> 3/19 (~1/6) to get straight on the river (you need J or 9).
You have straight draw on the flop, turn:
 - 1/7 -> 1/12 to get straight on the river (you need J).
Recomendation: Pay only one blind for the flop


Suited Connectors secret
You have suited connectors hand (5♦ 6♦) [2/17 (~1/9)].
 - 2/11 (~1/5) to get double straight or flush draw flop.
 - 3/10 (~2/7) to get straight or flush draw flop.
Recomendation: Pay a little bit more for the flop, but not a lot


Pair Secret
You have a pair hand (4♦ 4♥) [4/19 (~2/9) (~1/5)]:
 - 2/17 (~1/8) to get set on the flop.
You hava set on the flop, turn:
 - 5/17 (~2/7) -> 3/16 (~1/5) to get full house on the river.
 - 1/25 -> 1/50 to get four of a kind on the river.
Each ~1/7 you have a four of a kind instead of a full house on the river.
Recomendation: Strong but rare, try to get cheep flop. Catch staight or flush.




Table Straight Secret
The table roll 6 on a die get Double Straight Draw on flop (Somebody need 8♦ or J♥ to get straight). 5 players on flop.
The table roll 5 or 6 on a die at the flop get Straight Draw on flop (Somebody need 10♦ to get straight). 5 players on flop.
